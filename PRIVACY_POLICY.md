# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 5, 2026

## Introduction
Human Typer ("we", "our", or "the App") is a desktop application for Windows and macOS designed to simulate human typing behavior. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

## Data Collection
**Human Typer does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

-   **No Personal Information:** We do not collect names, email addresses, or any other personally identifiable information.
-   **No Usage Data:** We do not track how you use the application.
-   **No Network Activity:** The application runs entirely locally on your device and does not connect to the internet for any data reporting or analytics.

## Local Processing
All text processing and typing simulation occur locally on your machine. The text you input into the application remains in your computer's memory and is not sent to any external servers. The only file the App writes is a small local settings file (theme and slider preferences) stored in your operating system's standard application-settings directory; it contains no personal data and never leaves your device.
